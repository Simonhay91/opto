Company · 公司介绍

Who We Are

Optowire (Planet Fiber) is a leading manufacturer of fiber optic cables and network infrastructure products headquartered in Qingdao, China. With over 20 years of experience, we supply telecom operators, enterprises, and system integrators across 50+ countries.

Our product range covers everything from FTTH/FTTB drop cables and ODN components to complete data center cabling solutions and IoT devices.

Optowire（Planet Fiber）是一家总部位于中国青岛的光纤电缆及网络基础设施产品领先制造商。我们拥有超过20年的行业经验，为50多个国家的电信运营商、企业及系统集成商提供产品和服务。
